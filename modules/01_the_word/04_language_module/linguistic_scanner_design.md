# Linguistic Scanner Design

**Status:** Draft | **Author:** Brandon O'Shields

---

## Goal

The scanner detects language habits that reveal philosophical formation, then maps those detections into the seven-category diagnostic engine.

## Detection families

### 1. Metaphor detection

Look for recurring metaphors such as machine, brand, platform, movement, journey, ecosystem, product, pipeline, and empire. Metaphors often reveal metaphysics and social assumptions at once.

### 2. Pronoun pattern detection

Notice whether speech tends toward isolated first-person singular language, collectivized but abstract institutional language, or embodied communal language. Pronoun habits often illuminate anthropology and social imagination.

### 3. Identity language detection

Flag words such as authentic, true self, reinvent, platform, visibility, personal brand, and self-expression. These cues often map strongly to **Identity/Meaning** and **Anthropology**.

### 4. Institutional language detection

Flag phrases such as organizational health, stakeholder, alignment, strategic initiative, leadership pipeline, ministry product, and scaling strategy. These cues often map to **Social/Political**, **Ethics**, and **Metaphysics**.

### 5. Therapeutic language detection

Flag phrases such as hold space, protect my peace, emotional safety, trauma-informed above all, regulation, validation, and healing journey. These cues often map to **Anthropology**, **Ethics**, and **Identity/Meaning**.

### 6. Corporate language detection

Flag business-derived expressions such as vision casting, value proposition, engagement funnel, audience growth, optimization, and deliverables. These cues often map to **Social/Political** and **Ethics**.

### 7. Romanticized language detection

Flag idealized collective speech such as the Church is awakening, the Church must become who she is, or the movement is rising, especially when no embodied referent is named. These cues often map to **Social/Political**, **Metaphysics**, and **Identity/Meaning**.

## Category mapping model

### Metaphysics

Map language that describes what kind of thing reality is: machine, organism, platform, market, movement, idea.

### Epistemology

Map language that indicates how truth is accessed: I feel, data proves, experts say, my lived experience is final, no one can really know.

### Anthropology

Map language that reveals what a person is: self-author, wounded self, consumer, biological unit, image-bearer.

### Ethics

Map language that reveals what counts as good: safe, efficient, authentic, effective, faithful, holy.

### Social/Political

Map language that reveals how communities are imagined: institution, family, network, audience, ecosystem, covenant people.

### Identity/Meaning

Map language that reveals how the self is named and why life matters: platform, purpose, branding, belonging, calling, inheritance.

### Eschatology

Map language that reveals future hope: progress, breakthrough, optimization, healing arrival, resurrection, endurance.

## Output shape

Each detection should produce:

- the exact phrase or pattern,
- the primary category,
- the philosophical root,
- a covenantal alternative,
- optional notes if the phrase affects secondary categories.

## Design caution

The scanner should not assume that every flagged phrase is sinful or consciously ideological. It should surface possibilities for review, not issue verdicts automatically.
