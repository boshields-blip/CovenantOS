# Worldly vs Covenantal Dictionary

**Status:** Draft | **Owner:** @boshields-blip | **Author:** Brandon O'Shields | **Last updated:** 2026-08-01

---

The table below offers diagnostic examples rather than exhaustive replacements. The purpose is to make philosophical roots audible and to supply covenantal alternatives that better fit a relational, embodied, and faithful frame.

| Worldly phrase | Philosophical root | Covenantal alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Build the brand | Consumerism / corporate identity | Cultivate a faithful communal witness |
| Scale the ministry | Technocratic rationalism | Strengthen the work without outrunning people |
| Protect my truth | Expressive individualism | Speak truthfully before God and neighbor |
| I need to live my purpose | Expressive individualism | I need to receive and walk in my calling |
| This church is a machine | Technocratic rationalism | This congregation is a people under care |
| The Church needs a strategy refresh | Corporate institutionalism | Local churches need renewed faithfulness and wisdom |
| I’m deconstructing everything | Postmodern suspicion | I am testing what I have inherited and seeking what is true |
| We need a better product for young families | Consumerism | We need more faithful care for households |
| My identity is my story | Narrative individualism | My identity is received within God’s story |
| We need safe space above all else | Therapeutic anthropology | We need truthful, patient, and compassionate community |
| I’m curating my spiritual journey | Consumerism / expressive individualism | I am being formed in a covenant way of life |
| The church failed me, so I’m done | Institutional abstraction | Christians and leaders sinned against me, and I need truthful repair |
| We need thought leaders | Corporate elitism | We need wise and faithful teachers |
| Optimize the discipleship funnel | Technocratic rationalism | Shepherd people patiently toward maturity |
| I’m following what gives me life | Therapeutic individualism | I am seeking what is faithful and life-giving in Christ |
| The institution must survive | Institutional self-preservation | The people must be shepherded in truth |
| Our mission is market capture | Consumerism | Our task is faithful witness and service |
| I need to protect my peace | Therapeutic inwardness | I need to pursue peace with truth and wisdom |
| We need a platform | Influence culture | We need trustworthy presence and testimony |
| The right side of history | Progressivist utopianism | Faithfulness before God across history |
| This is my season to shine | Expressive individualism | This is a season to serve faithfully |
| The church should meet my needs | Consumerism | I belong to a people where we bear one another’s burdens |
| I need to reinvent myself | Expressive individualism | I need repentance, renewal, and growth |
| We need culture wins | Progressivist / tribal politics | We need faithful presence and truthful endurance |
| I’m managing my spiritual wellness | Therapeutic anthropology | I am tending my soul before God in embodied life |
| We must maximize engagement | Corporate analytics culture | We must deepen participation and faithfulness |
| Our people are stakeholders | Corporate institutionalism | Our people are members of a covenant community |
| I need authenticity, not authority | Expressive individualism | I need truthful authority and honest fellowship |
| We’re pivoting the ministry model | Startup logic | We are discerning wiser practices for this people |
| The church needs better optics | Brand management | The church needs visible integrity |
