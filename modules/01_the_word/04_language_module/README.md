## Purpose

This layer identifies how worldly philosophical assumptions are embedded in Christian speech and offers covenantal alternatives that can retrain imagination rather than merely correct vocabulary.

## Why language matters

Language does not simply describe reality; it tutors perception. A church that constantly speaks in corporate, therapeutic, romantic, or consumer terms will slowly imagine God, community, authority, and hope in those same terms.

## Four components

### 1. Scanner

Detects key phrases, metaphors, pronoun patterns, institutional abstractions, and therapeutic cues.

### 2. Diagnostic Engine

Maps detected language to underlying philosophical roots and the seven canonical categories.

### 3. Covenantal Language Generator

Suggests alternative phrasings that better reflect a relational, embodied, covenantal frame.

### 4. Formation Pathway

Turns repeated language patterns into practical invitations for repentance, renewal, and new habits of speech.

## Posture

This module is not a legalistic speech police. It is a discernment aid. Many people speak borrowed language without malicious intent. The aim is gentle clarification that opens a better imagination.
