## Name

**Module name:** Materialism

## Description

Materialism treats matter, energy, and measurable processes as the only or final reality. Spiritual claims are reduced to brain states, social functions, or private projections.

## Category-by-category summary

### 1. Metaphysics

- Core claim: only material processes are ultimately real.
- Diagnostic signal: moral and spiritual language is translated into mechanistic explanation.
- Notes: transcendence disappears or becomes decorative.

### 2. Epistemology

- Core claim: knowledge is trustworthy only when empirically measurable or scientifically inferred.
- Diagnostic signal: revelation and testimony are treated as inferior knowledge forms.
- Notes: the range of valid knowing becomes unnecessarily narrow.

### 3. Anthropology

- Core claim: humans are biological organisms shaped by chemistry, instinct, and environment.
- Diagnostic signal: soul, vocation, and holiness are replaced by behavior, cognition, and survival terms.
- Notes: dignity becomes difficult to ground without borrowing from non-material frames.

### 4. Ethics

- Core claim: morality is either adaptive behavior or social convention.
- Diagnostic signal: moral norms are justified only by utility or social stability.
- Notes: goodness loses transcendental weight.

### 5. Social/Political

- Core claim: communities are systems for managing resources, bodies, and interests.
- Diagnostic signal: covenant, sacred obligation, and worship are treated as secondary overlays.
- Notes: society becomes functional rather than holy.

### 6. Identity/Meaning

- Core claim: meaning is self-created against an indifferent universe.
- Diagnostic signal: purpose is framed as personal project or legacy management.
- Notes: the self is burdened with inventing significance.

### 7. Eschatology

- Core claim: history has no given telos beyond survival, continuation, or extinction.
- Diagnostic signal: hope shrinks to lifespan management, social legacy, or species continuity.
- Notes: resurrection hope is unintelligible on strictly material terms.

## Distortions this module often produces

- It hollows out transcendence and worship while still relying on borrowed moral language.
- It narrows human dignity to measurable functions.
- It tempts Christians to live as practical atheists in everyday life.

## Strengths or truths it often notices

- It takes bodily reality and physical causation seriously.
- It resists vague spiritualism detached from actual creation.
- It values careful observation of the material world.

## Covenantal contrast

The covenantal philosophy affirms the goodness of the material world without reducing reality to matter alone. Bodies matter because creation is real, but bodies are not all that is real. Human life is received from God and ordered toward communion, holiness, and renewal.
