# Western Christianity Diagnostic Map

**Status:** Draft | **Owner:** @boshields-blip | **Author:** Brandon O'Shields | **Last updated:** 2026-08-01

---

This map summarizes the "big reveal": Western Christians often confess biblical doctrine while living from a mixed philosophical inheritance. The result is not usually outright apostasy, but layered formation — where Christian beliefs are carried in frameworks shaped by rival stories.

## Category map

| Category | Common absorbed philosophies | Resulting Christian distortion | Covenantal diagnosis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Metaphysics | Materialism, technocratic rationalism, progressivist utopianism | Reality is treated as neutral stuff, system, or historical machine rather than meaningful creation | Recover creation as gift, order, and theater of covenantal responsibility |
| Epistemology | Rationalism, therapeutic inwardness, postmodern suspicion | Truth is reduced to detached analysis, private feeling, or endless critique | Recover revelation, obedience, communal discernment, and historical memory |
| Anthropology | Expressive individualism, therapeutic anthropology, consumerism | The self becomes sovereign, fragile, or market-shaped instead of covenantally called | Recover the human person as image-bearer, steward, neighbor, and worshiper |
| Ethics | Consumerism, therapeutic anthropology, pragmatism, technocratic rationalism | Goodness becomes satisfaction, safety, or efficiency instead of covenant fidelity | Recover holiness, justice, repentance, and truthful love |
| Social/Political | Liberal individualism, bureaucratic technocracy, market logic, utopian activism | Church and society become either contract, platform, or programmatic machine | Recover accountable communities under God rather than systems of management or abstraction |
| Identity/Meaning | Expressive individualism, consumerism, postmodern narrative identity | Identity becomes self-authored, branded, or perpetually revised | Recover identity as received within God’s story and covenant people |
| Eschatology | Progressivist utopianism, consumer upgrade culture, therapeutic arrival | Hope shifts to reform success, endless improvement, or inner closure | Recover resurrectional hope, patient faithfulness, and new creation |

## Category-by-category reveal

### 1. Metaphysics

**Absorbed influences:** materialism, system-thinking without theology, progress myths.

**Resulting distortion:** Christians may speak biblically but act as though the real world is governed by markets, metrics, and material causation alone.

**Covenantal diagnosis:** The world must be re-seen as creation: ordered, gifted, morally significant, and interpreted in Christ.

### 2. Epistemology

**Absorbed influences:** Enlightenment rationalism, expressive inwardness, postmodern suspicion.

**Resulting distortion:** Some Christians treat doctrine as a detached intellectual grid, others treat authenticity as truth, and others distrust any confident claim.

**Covenantal diagnosis:** Truth is known through revelation, faithful reasoning, obedient practice, and communal discernment.

### 3. Anthropology

**Absorbed influences:** expressive individualism, therapeutic anthropology, consumer anthropology.

**Resulting distortion:** The believer becomes a sovereign chooser, a managed wounded self, or a consumer of religious goods.

**Covenantal diagnosis:** Human beings are image-bearers called into communion, discipline, stewardship, and holiness.

### 4. Ethics

**Absorbed influences:** therapeutic moralism, consumerism, pragmatism, efficiency culture.

**Resulting distortion:** Faithfulness is quietly displaced by felt safety, personal fulfillment, or visible success.

**Covenantal diagnosis:** The good is covenant fidelity expressed in love, justice, truth, repentance, and holy action.

### 5. Social/Political

**Absorbed influences:** liberal individualism, bureaucratic management, corporate logic, utopian political hope.

**Resulting distortion:** Congregations are imagined as service providers, brands, or strategic delivery systems; "the Church" becomes a personified institution.

**Covenantal diagnosis:** Communities must be re-understood as embodied, accountable covenant life under God, not as abstract corporate actors.

### 6. Identity/Meaning

**Absorbed influences:** expressive individualism, postmodern identity theory, consumer curation.

**Resulting distortion:** Meaning becomes self-authorship, platform, visibility, or alignment with a chosen tribe.

**Covenantal diagnosis:** Identity is received within God’s story, clarified through vocation, and sustained in the communion of saints.

### 7. Eschatology

**Absorbed influences:** progressivist utopianism, upgrade culture, therapeutic arrival.

**Resulting distortion:** Christians expect final resolution from cultural progress, institutional wins, or personal healing breakthroughs.

**Covenantal diagnosis:** Hope must be relocated in Christ’s return, resurrection, judgment, and new creation, which frees present obedience from panic and illusion.
