# Closing — Come to the Table

## Purpose

This is the short liturgy for movement 7 (*Bless and send*) — the closing blessing said as plates are cleared. It marks the end of the session and releases the household into the rest of the evening.

---

## The closing words

*Said aloud by the host, or together:*

> May the God we have heard make us people who live in light of what is true.
> Go in peace.

---

## Notes for the host

The closing is brief by design. It marks the boundary between the session and what comes after — the clearing of the table, the rest of the evening, the rest of the week.

Do not add a summary after the closing. Do not return to a point that was not resolved during the session. The closing is the closing. Let it end.

If the household has a natural phrase it already uses at the end of a meal or a prayer — *"Go in peace," "Amen," "The Lord be with you"* — that phrase belongs here. The Come to the Table practice is intentionally adaptable.

---

## The live invitation as a send

Some households find it helpful to name the live invitation again at the close — a short, spoken reminder of the one thing the household is carrying into the week.

If this is done, it should be brief:

> *"This week: [live invitation]. Amen."*

This is optional, not canonical. The canonical closing is simply the blessing.
