# Opening — Come to the Table

## Purpose

This is the short liturgy for movement 1 (*Gather*) and movement 2 (*Bless the meal*) — the call to attention and the blessing of the food. It is said aloud, together when possible.

It is designed to be:
- Brief enough not to feel ceremonial
- Simple enough for a young child to say with the host
- Translation-agnostic
- Repeatable week after week without becoming empty

---

## The opening words

*The host calls the table to attention:*

> We come to this table.
> The meal is a gift.
> The words are a gift.
> Let us eat and hear together.

*Pause. Then the blessing of the meal:*

> God, You made this food. You made us hungry. You made us to eat together. Thank You.
> Amen.

---

## Notes for the host

These words are offered as a starting point, not a fixed text. A household may develop its own opening over time. The important things are:

- Something marks the transition from ordinary time to the session.
- The meal is named as a gift, not an assumption.
- Children can participate — even a toddler can say "Amen."

If the household has an established meal blessing, use it. The Come to the Table practice does not require a new liturgy to replace an existing one; it invites what is already present to be placed consciously in relation to Scripture.
