# Seasonal Liturgy — Come to the Table

**Status:** Draft | **Author:** Brandon O'Shields

---

Come to the Table keeps its canonical weekly seven-movement shape:

**Gather → Bless → Read → Notice → Know → Live → Bless and Send**

The structure does not change by season. What changes seasonally is:

1. the blessing at meal,
2. the closing blessing,
3. an optional seasonal framing for *Live*, and
4. the thread that ties the weekly household table to the nearby quarterly [Harvest & Hymn](/the-word/living-the-covenant/harvest-and-hymn) gathering.

---

## Why a seasonal overlay

The seasonal overlay allows households to keep one continuous rhythm:

- weekly table practice at home,
- quarterly gathering in community.

This keeps weekly formation and community worship aligned without complicating the household session.
