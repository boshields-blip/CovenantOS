# Family Preparation — Harvest & Hymn

---

A household does not arrive at Harvest & Hymn empty-handed, and preparing for it is itself a small formation practice, not a chore to check off before the real thing starts. Families prepare through a simple three-part rhythm in the weeks before each gathering: worship, homestead work, and reflection.

## 1. Family worship

In the weeks before the gathering, take up the season's anchor passages the same way you would at [Come to the Table](/the-word/living-the-covenant/come-to-the-table): read one aloud together, sing and pray in simple repeatable forms, and choose one creative offering to prepare as a household.

### Seasonal creative prompts

- **Spring:** Write a short psalm of new beginnings.
- **Summer:** Create a chant or melody about daily faithfulness.
- **Fall:** Write a thanksgiving poem or testimony.
- **Winter:** Compose a reflective prayer or quiet song.

## 2. Homestead work

Every season has its own ordinary labor, and the offering a household brings should grow out of that labor rather than be invented separately from it:

- Spring: planting and planning
- Summer: tending and perseverance
- Fall: harvesting and preserving
- Winter: mending and preparing

A household that does not farm or garden can still ask what the season's equivalent labor looks like in their own life and bring that instead. The point is not agricultural literacy. It is letting the actual rhythm of the season shape what gets brought to the table.

## 3. Reflection and table-share prompts

Before the gathering, discuss as a family:

- What has God taught us this season?
- What fruit have we seen in our household?
- What can we bring to strengthen others?

Bring one table-share answer to the gathering itself:

- **Spring:** What are you planting this year?
- **Summer:** What are you tending faithfully right now?
- **Fall:** What are you harvesting — physically or spiritually?
- **Winter:** What is God teaching you in the quiet season?
