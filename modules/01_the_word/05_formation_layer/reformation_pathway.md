# Re-formation Pathway

This document is a brief stub for the future formation layer.

## Formation loop

The Word intends to move from diagnosis to renewal through a repeating loop:

1. **Put off** — identify false gods, false stories, false speech, and false habits.
2. **Renew the mind** — return to covenant truth, Scripture, memory, and rightly ordered imagination.
3. **Put on** — practice covenantal habits, truthful language, embodied obedience, and communal faithfulness.

A parallel summary of the same movement is:

- **Identify false gods**
- **Return to covenant**
- **Walk in the way**

## Planned components

Future work is expected to expand this layer with:

- practices,
- liturgies,
- discernment tools,
- covenant habits,
- narrative re-grounding,
- communal exercises,
- language retraining routines.

## First concrete communal practice — Come to the Table

The first implementation of the formation loop's *communal exercises* bucket is now scaffolded under:

```text
05_formation_layer/come_to_the_table/
```

**Come to the Table** is a weekly dinner practice. A household gathers at the table, blesses the meal, reads a short passage aloud from an actual Bible, talks about what they heard, learns one thing from the text, and ends with simple prayer and blessing.

It is designed for households with children across a wide range of ages — from an infant who hears the words while held to a teenager ready to trace covenantal philosophy — and it is designed to require almost no prep burden beyond faithfulness and presence.

See [come_to_the_table/README.md](./come_to_the_table/README.md) for the full module description and navigation.

## Quarterly community gathering — Harvest & Hymn

The second formation module in this layer extends the same rhythm into shared seasonal gathering:

```text
05_formation_layer/harvest_and_hymn/
```

**Harvest & Hymn** is a quarterly, four-season community gathering where households bring shared meal, creative offerings, and testimony under the ordering text of 1 Corinthians 14:26.

Together, the modules are designed as one cadence:

- weekly household table practice in [come_to_the_table/README.md](./come_to_the_table/README.md)
- quarterly community gathering in [harvest_and_hymn/README.md](./harvest_and_hymn/README.md)
