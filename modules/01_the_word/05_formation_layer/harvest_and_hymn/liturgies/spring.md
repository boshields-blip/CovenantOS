# Spring Liturgy — Planting & Promise

## Arrival blessing

> Bless the seeds and the hands that sow them.
> Bless this table as a place of promise and patient hope.

## Meal-share prompt

What are you planting this year, and what hope are you trusting God to grow?

## Creative prompt

Offer a short psalm, poem, melody, drawing, or testimony about new beginnings.

## Elder's teaching seed

"Sow righteousness; reap steadfast love."

## Sending blessing

> Go and sow in hope.
