# Summer Liturgy — Growth & Faithfulness

## Arrival blessing

> Bless the work of tending and the patience of daily obedience.
> Keep us abiding in You as branches in the Vine.

## Meal-share prompt

What are you tending faithfully right now, even when growth feels slow?

## Creative prompt

Offer a chant, song, testimony, drawing, or reading about steady faithfulness.

## Elder's teaching seed

"Abide in the Vine."

## Sending blessing

> Go and grow in faithfulness.
