# Winter Liturgy — Rest & Reflection

## Arrival blessing

> Bless the stillness of this season and teach us holy rest.
> In quietness and trust, steady our hearts.

## Meal-share prompt

What is God teaching your household in the quiet season?

## Creative prompt

Offer a quiet melody, reflective prayer, story, drawing, or testimony of trust.

## Elder's teaching seed

"In returning and rest you shall be saved."

## Sending blessing

> Go and rest in His peace.
