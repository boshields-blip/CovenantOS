# Gathering Format — Harvest & Hymn

**Status:** Draft | **Author:** Brandon O'Shields

---

## Canonical quarterly shape

Every Harvest & Hymn gathering follows the same five movements. Structure remains stable; seasonal content changes.

**Target total time:** ~1 hour 45 minutes to 2 hours 30 minutes, including meal.

---

## A. Arrival & Table Setting (15–20 minutes)

Families arrive, place food, and help set the shared table. A short seasonal arrival blessing is spoken to mark the gathering.

## B. Shared Meal (45–60 minutes)

Households share what they brought and name one thing the season taught them. This is both nourishment and testimony.

## C. Creative Offerings (30–45 minutes)

Families and children bring seasonal offerings: psalm, poem, song, chant, melody, drawing, testimony, reading, or prayer.

## D. Elder's Equipping Moment (10–15 minutes)

The elder gives a brief seasonal teaching, names one concrete household practice for the coming quarter, and blesses the assembly.

## E. Closing Blessing & Sending (5 minutes)

A short seasonal sending concludes the gathering and sends households back to weekly table life.
