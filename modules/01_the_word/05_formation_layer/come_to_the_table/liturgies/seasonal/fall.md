# Fall Seasonal Overlay — Come to the Table

> Giver of every good gift, bless this meal and the fruits of this season.
> Teach us gratitude that becomes generosity.

> May the Lord make you rich in thanksgiving and ready to share.
> Go in peace, and give thanks in abundance.
