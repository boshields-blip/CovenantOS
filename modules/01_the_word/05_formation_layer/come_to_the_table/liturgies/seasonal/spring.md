# Spring Seasonal Overlay — Come to the Table

## Opening blessing

> God of promise, bless this meal and the work of sowing before us.
> Teach us to plant in hope and to trust Your faithfulness.

## Closing blessing

> May the Lord establish what is planted in faith.
> Go in peace, and sow in hope.
