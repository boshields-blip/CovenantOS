# Come to the Table

**Status:** Draft | **Author:** Brandon O'Shields

---

> *A weekly meal, a short passage, and a household learning to know God together.*

**Come to the Table** is the first concrete communal practice under the Formation Layer of The Word. It gives a household a simple, repeatable liturgy for sharing a meal and sharing the Scriptures at the same table.

> **This directory is documentation-first.** It lives under `modules/01_the_word/05_formation_layer/come_to_the_table/` and does not define app code or persistence wiring.

---

## What this module is

Come to the Table is a weekly dinner practice. Once a week, a household gathers at a table, blesses the meal, reads a short passage aloud from an actual Bible, talks about what they heard, learns one thing from the text, and ends with simple prayer and blessing.

That is the whole shape. It takes roughly ten to fifteen minutes embedded inside a normal meal — not added on top of one.

Over years of practice, a family that keeps this table will absorb the authors and languages of Scripture, the canonical story from Genesis through Revelation, the seven covenantal philosophical categories, and the posture of attentive hearing — all without turning the meal into school.

---

## Who it is for

Any household willing to open a Bible at the table. The format is designed for families with children spanning a wide range of ages — from an infant who hears the words while held, to a teenager ready to trace covenantal philosophy.

It is also designed to be hospitable outward: a format portable enough to share with another family, simple enough that a guest family can follow it without preparation, and generous enough that it can adapt to smaller children, older saints, and uneven schedules.

## How to start

1. Read [SESSION_FORMAT.md](/the-word/formation/come-to-the-table/session-format) — the canonical seven-movement liturgy of one weekly meal.
2. Read [AGE_BANDS.md](/the-word/formation/come-to-the-table/age-bands) — how to invite each age into the same passage.
3. Run the first session using the pattern in [SESSION_FORMAT.md](/the-word/formation/come-to-the-table/session-format).
4. Use the [HOST_GUIDE.md](/the-word/formation/come-to-the-table/host-guide) to prepare and lead.
5. When you are ready to think about the longer plan, read [CANONICAL_ARC.md](/the-word/formation/come-to-the-table/canonical-arc).

There is no required reading before the first session. Pick a passage, open a Bible, and begin.

## How this practice relates to the rest of The Word

- **01 Foundational Layer** supplies the covenantal "who God is" that the *Live* movement points to every week.
- **02 Diagnostic Layer** quietly underwrites the *Know* slot's "philosophy point" — the seven categories appear at the table without being named as a curriculum.
- **03 Berean Tool** is brought to the table occasionally, with guardrails, as part of the Apprentice stage. See [BEREAN_AT_THE_TABLE.md](/the-word/formation/come-to-the-table/berean-at-the-table).
- **04 Language Module** feeds the "language note" and helps the household hear how Scripture's own vocabulary reshapes imagination.
- **05 Formation Layer** — this module is the first concrete instance of the communal exercise promised in [reformation_pathway.md](/the-word/formation/reformation-pathway).

## Seasonal & Community

Come to the Table remains the weekly household practice. The seasonal overlay in [SEASONAL_LITURGY.md](/the-word/formation/come-to-the-table/seasonal-liturgy) keeps that weekly rhythm aligned with the quarterly community gathering in [../harvest_and_hymn/README.md](/the-word/formation/harvest-and-hymn).

Together they form one cadence: weekly at home, quarterly in community.
