## Reflection questions for the reader

1. What in this analysis surprised you — about the teacher, or about yourself?
2. Where do you share this teacher's strengths? Where do you share their drifts?
3. What did you learn about the tradition this teacher comes from?
4. How would you describe this teacher charitably to someone who has never heard of them?
5. Has this analysis increased or decreased your ability to receive this teacher's genuine goods?

---

## Closing caution

This profile should never be used as a verdict on a person's sincerity or salvation. It is a structured aid for discernment, humility, and better listening. The purpose of naming a drift is not to diminish a person, but to increase clarity about what kind of formation is taking place.
