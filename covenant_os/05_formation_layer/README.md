## Purpose

The Formation Layer contains the embodied practices through which the principles, diagnostics, tools, and language of the four lower layers are lived out in community. Formation is not primarily intellectual — it is practiced, repeated, and embodied. You do not become formed by understanding formation; you become formed by doing the practices that shape you over time.

This layer is where CovenantOS becomes most tangible. It is where content becomes rhythm, and rhythm becomes character. The Formation Layer draws freely on all four layers below it and is the only layer in the system that is permitted to reference any other layer.

## What belongs here

- **Come to the Table** — a liturgical and relational practice for community gathering, honest examination, and mutual recommitment. The practice creates space for communities to bring what is actually true — including failure, grief, and conflict — into a structured, safe, and hopeful setting.
- **Harvest and Hymn** — a seasonal practice that connects the rhythms of creation (planting, tending, harvesting, resting) with the rhythms of personal and community formation. It uses the physical reality of a harvest season as a lens for examining what has grown, what has died, and what is being prepared for the next season.
- **Practice guides:** step-by-step facilitation guides for each practice, including timing, roles, materials, and facilitator notes
- **Community rhythm templates:** seasonal, weekly, or periodic schedules for integrating formation practices into community life
- **Facilitator formation resources:** content specifically for those who lead formation practices — how to hold space well, how to handle difficulty, how to connect practices to the lower layers

## Connection to lower layers

The Formation Layer is the culmination of the full 5-layer stack. Its practices are grounded in the first principles of Layer 1, illuminated by the diagnostic frameworks of Layer 2, enriched by the textual engagement of Layer 3, and spoken in the vocabulary of Layer 4. None of the practices in this layer make full sense in isolation from the layers below.
