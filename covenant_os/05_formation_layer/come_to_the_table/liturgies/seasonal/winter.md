# Winter Seasonal Overlay — Come to the Table

## Opening blessing

> God of peace, bless this table and quiet our striving.
> Teach us to trust You in returning and rest.

## Closing blessing

> May the Lord give you rest of heart and steadiness of hope.
> Go in peace, and rest in His peace.
