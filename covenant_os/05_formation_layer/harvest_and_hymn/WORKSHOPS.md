# Workshops — Harvest & Hymn

Optional workshops may be offered before or after the canonical gathering format. They are supplementary and should not replace the five liturgical movements.

## Spring

- Seed starting
- Composting
- Psalm-writing for kids

## Summer

- Herbal medicine
- Songwriting
- Garden tours

## Fall

- Canning
- Bread baking
- Harvest psalm night

## Winter

- Mending and crafting
- Storytelling
- Quiet worship nights
