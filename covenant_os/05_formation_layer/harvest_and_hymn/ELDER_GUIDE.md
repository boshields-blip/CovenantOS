# Elder Guide — Harvest & Hymn

## Role of the elder

The elder equips the gathering but does not dominate it. The community's shared contributions remain central.

At each gathering, the elder offers:

1. A concise seasonal teaching
2. A practical household practice to carry into the quarter
3. A final blessing for sending

---

## Seasonal teaching examples

- **Spring:** "Sow righteousness; reap steadfast love."
- **Summer:** "Abide in the Vine."
- **Fall:** "He gives seed to the sower and bread to the eater."
- **Winter:** "In returning and rest you shall be saved."

These are teaching seeds, not full sermons. Keep teaching short enough that households can remember and practice it.

---

## Practices for non-dominating leadership

- Keep the equipping moment within the agreed time.
- Name and affirm family and child contributions publicly.
- Invite quiet households without pressure.
- Resist turning the gathering into a lecture hall.
- End with blessing, not extended commentary.
