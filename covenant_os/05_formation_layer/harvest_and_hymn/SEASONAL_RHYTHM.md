# Seasonal Rhythm — Harvest & Hymn

Harvest & Hymn follows a four-season annual pattern. The gathering shape stays stable, but content, prompts, and emphasis shift with the season.

## Spring — Planting & Promise

- **Scripture anchors:** Genesis 8:22; Hosea 10:12; Psalm 1
- **Theme:** beginnings, sowing, hope, trust
- **Creative tone:** simple, fresh, expectant
- **Homestead tie-ins:** seed starting, soil preparation, planning, first growth

## Summer — Growth & Faithfulness

- **Scripture anchors:** John 15; Galatians 6:9; Psalm 104
- **Theme:** abiding, tending, perseverance, steady obedience
- **Creative tone:** joyful, rhythmic, energetic
- **Homestead tie-ins:** garden care, animal tending, long days of labor, patient maintenance

## Fall — Harvest & Thanksgiving

- **Scripture anchors:** Psalm 67; Deuteronomy 8; 2 Corinthians 9
- **Theme:** abundance, gratitude, generosity, offering
- **Creative tone:** communal, celebratory, reflective
- **Homestead tie-ins:** harvest, preserving, shared feasting, storage

## Winter — Rest & Reflection

- **Scripture anchors:** Psalm 46; Isaiah 30:15; Hebrews 4
- **Theme:** stillness, remembrance, trust, preparation
- **Creative tone:** quiet, contemplative, acoustic
- **Homestead tie-ins:** mending, planning, storytelling, Sabbath-like slowing
