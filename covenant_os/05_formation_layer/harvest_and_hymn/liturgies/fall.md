# Fall Liturgy — Harvest & Thanksgiving

## Arrival blessing

> Bless the harvest and the hands that gathered it.
> Teach us gratitude that overflows into generosity.

## Meal-share prompt

What are you harvesting this season — physically or spiritually?

## Creative prompt

Offer a thanksgiving psalm, poem, testimony, song, or family reflection.

## Elder's teaching seed

"He gives seed to the sower and bread to the eater."

## Sending blessing

> Go and give thanks in abundance.
