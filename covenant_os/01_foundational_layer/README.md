## Purpose

The Foundational Layer holds the first principles and operating frame of CovenantOS. It is the bedrock on which every other layer rests. Nothing in this layer depends on any other layer — it is the one place in the system that stands alone.

Content here articulates the core assumptions about personhood, covenant, community, formation, and truth that orient everything built above. These are not talking points or marketing language; they are the actual philosophical and theological commitments that give CovenantOS its shape.

If a question arises in any higher layer about why something is the way it is, the answer should trace back to a principle in this layer.

## What belongs here

- Core convictions about personhood: what a human being is, what human dignity requires, how persons are known and formed
- Core convictions about covenant: what it means to make and keep promises, what covenant community looks like, and how covenantal obligations are understood
- The operating frame for formation: why formation matters, what it aims at, and what it means for formation to succeed or fail
- Definitions of key terms at their most fundamental level, before the Language Module elaborates them for practical use

## What does not belong here

This layer does not contain diagnostic tools (Layer 2), scripture study templates (Layer 3), vocabulary guides (Layer 4), or practice liturgies (Layer 5). It contains only the first-order commitments that make those tools and practices coherent.
