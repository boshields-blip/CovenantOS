# Covenant OS

**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** @boshields-blip  
**Last updated:** 2026-06-18

---

**CovenantOS** is a philosophical and formational tool that helps individuals and communities name, diagnose, and reform the assumptions that shape Christian imagination and practice.

## What lives here

Covenant OS is organized into five integrated layers:

1. **Foundational Layer** — the covenantal philosophy that supplies the governing frame
2. **Diagnostic Engine** — a seven-category engine for examining rival philosophies and distortions
3. **Berean Tool** — a scripture and text examination tool that applies the diagnostic engine to real-world influences
4. **Language Module** — the bridge that exposes how language carries philosophy and reshapes imagination
5. **Formation Layer** — the re-formation pathway that turns diagnosis into embodied renewal

## Intended posture

This content assumes a tone that is truthful, gentle, precise, and covenantal. It is designed to produce clarity without hostility, discernment without division, and formation without fear. Where diagnosis names distortions, the aim is not condemnation but repentance, reordering, and hope.
