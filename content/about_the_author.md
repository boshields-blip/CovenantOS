# About the Author

## A short introduction

CovenantOS is a guided, covenantal atlas — an invitation to re-see life through the world of Scripture, where the Word forms our imagination, the household becomes the first church, and neighbors relearn how to see one another face to face. It is not a program or a platform. It is the overflow of one man's journey with the Lord, offered to you in hope.

My name is Brandon O'Shields, and I am the author. Let me tell you a little of where this came from.

## My journey

I love Jesus. The Holy Spirit began His work in me when I was eight years old, and it has been a long walk since — not a straight road, but a real one, with wandering and wrestling and mercy all along the way. For much of that walk I have struggled to *fit* into the church contexts I found myself in. I have served on staff at a church, helped plant a church, led a transitional housing ministry, and led a home fellowship. I came up in the Southern Baptist tradition, and I am grateful for it — and I have also come to love and learn from many other streams of the Christian family.

Somewhere in the middle of all this, I came apart. I went through a genuine crisis of faith, and I questioned a great deal of what I had always believed. Was any of it true? Had I built my life on something solid, or on sand? I have come to believe that questioning is good — we are meant to wrestle honestly with what we have been handed — but I learned something hard in the middle of it: *there is a difference between questioning your tradition and deconstructing it.* One refines; the other demolishes. I did not want to tear down the house I was raised in. I wanted to know what was true underneath it, and whether Christ was really there.

Out of the depths of that season, I wrote a lament — a raw, honest prayer that moved from my pain to the sight of the crucified and risen Christ. If you want to see where this journey took me on my knees, you can read [A Lament](/the-word/living-the-covenant/a-lament).

What helped me most, strangely, was the land. For twenty years I have worked as a plumber and a business owner, and along the way I have raised up other plumbers in my community — which has taught me more about discipleship than I expected, because you cannot hand a man a trade from a distance. You have to get down in the mud and work beside him. I am also a farmer. I tend sheep and cattle and chickens, and I have a few fig trees I am learning to keep. And when I began to eat the food from my own garden and my own farm, I started to see the world differently.

What I saw in my own life was, honestly, alarming — how far I had drifted from the land, from my food, from my neighbors, from the very things Scripture simply assumes. I had been living in a world cut into pieces, and I had never noticed. That sent me on a journey to understand God, land, people, and the Bible as one connected whole instead of separate compartments. And the more I walked in the real world of soil and season and household, the more the world of Scripture opened to me — because it turns out they are the same world. CovenantOS is the overflow of that walk.

But let me be plain about what matters most. My greatest ambition and my deepest joy is not this project. It is loving my wife and raising my children in the Lord. Everything here grows out of that ordinary, covenantal life — the table, the household, the land, the neighbor. That is where I am actually being formed.

## Where I stand, and what I hope for you

I believe the covenant-household way of living fits *within* all of the Christian traditions, not over against them. It is not a new denomination, and it is not a claim that everyone else has gotten it wrong. It is a way of living and serving faithfully in our own contexts — a way that promotes unity as we wrestle with the Scriptures together and ask the deep questions honestly, without letting go of one another. My hope is simple: that CovenantOS would encourage you to challenge your beliefs without deconstructing your tradition, and to immerse yourself in the world of Scripture, until you come to know more of our Creator and Redeemer — who revealed Himself through His prophets and apostles, and finally and most fully through His Son, Jesus Christ.

## How this was made

I want to be honest about how this came together. The worldview, the structure, and the convictions here are my own, worked out over years of study, labor, and life. Rather than hiring editors, researchers, and consultants, I used AI as a tool — to help me research, draft, sharpen, and stress-test the writing, and lately to help me build the software behind this atlas, the same way I am learning to use these tools in my plumbing business. My wife has been my first reader all along. I directed the whole of it and made every judgment about what belonged and what did not. The convictions are mine, and I stand behind them.

I say this openly for the same reason the rest of CovenantOS names its lens openly: you deserve to know whose eyes you are looking through, and to know that a real man — with a wife, children, a trade, and a farm — stands behind these words and can be held to account for them.

Grace and peace to you as you walk.

*— Brandon O'Shields*
