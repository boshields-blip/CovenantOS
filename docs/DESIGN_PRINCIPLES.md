# Design Principles

**Status:** Draft | **Owner:** @boshields-blip | **Last updated:** 2026-07-31

---

## Purpose

These principles define the design language for CovenantOS 2.0. They describe not only how the interface should look and behave, but how the product should think: slowly, relationally, and covenantally.

---

## Hebraic cognition as design language

CovenantOS should privilege **walking over abstract thinking**. The experience should feel like entering a land, tracing a path, dwelling in a household, and standing inside an inheritance rather than consuming disconnected content blocks.

This leads to five governing contrasts:

- **Walking over thinking** — movement, sequence, and encounter over detached abstraction
- **Practices over abstractions** — embodied rhythms and lived questions over mere concepts
- **Household over individual** — formation is social and inherited, not merely private
- **Land over digital space** — place, boundary, season, and stewardship matter
- **Covenant over contract** — fidelity, belonging, and inheritance are prior to exchange

---

## No-backend principle

CovenantOS 2.0 is intentionally static and interactive. It has no accounts, no forum, no membership, no Supabase dependency, and no requirement for server-side persistence in the target architecture. Interaction should deepen reflection without introducing platform behaviors that depend on authentication, moderation, or social feeds.

When a user is ready to move from formation to participation, CovenantOS should point outward to CommunityOS rather than absorbing bookings, listings, account flows, or transactional coordination into this product.

---

## Interactive web-first

The product is a guided covenantal atlas, not a dashboard or feed. Interface patterns should favor pathways, maps, layered cards, comparisons, and reflective prompts over notifications, timelines, or productivity chrome.

Interactivity exists to help users:
- compare rival frames
- move through a sequence
- reveal context gradually
- test a contrast or relationship
- articulate a response in the moment

---

## Content-first

Markdown content drives CovenantOS. Structure, naming, and module sequencing should emerge from the content itself, and any future UI should serve that structure rather than forcing the content into an application-first mold.

In practice, this means:
- content architecture comes before component architecture
- module and layer naming should remain intelligible in plain text
- the interface should illuminate the content, not compete with it

---

## Module flow rationale

The sequence **The Word → The Household → The Compact** is not arbitrary.

- **The Word** forms imagination, language, and covenantal perception
- **The Household** embodies that imagination in stewardship, labor, hospitality, land, and economy
- **The Compact** emerges from rightly ordered households and shared life rather than from institutional abstractions alone

The order resists a modern inversion that tries to repair politics without repairing households, and to repair households without first reforming imagination.
